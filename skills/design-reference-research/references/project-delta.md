# Project Delta: what to research for each part

Project Delta (working name) is the user's portfolio builder. People in product, design, analysis and engineering upload their material, get a drafted case study, edit it, and publish a portfolio to their own address. Read this only when the design work is for this product.

## What the user has said about the current design

- The builder feels clunky and heavy; using it takes too long and "gives no vibe".
- Pages feel "dead" and it does not look like a SaaS product.
- Every block on a public page styles its own heading, so pages do not read as one design.
- The user wants themes that other users can pick and customise, including decorators and motion.
- Recruiters skim. A case study must make its point in under a minute, with depth below.

Use these as the test for any reference: does borrowing from it make the product feel lighter, more alive, and more like one coherent design?

## The four surfaces

### 1. Builder: app.projectdelta.com

Screens: dashboard, project and blog lists, project editor, new-project flow with the assistant, block picker, page editors, media library, settings (address and domain, theme, credits and plan), Knowledge Base, Interview Studio, sign-up and onboarding.

Research questions:

- How do the best editors let people edit on the page instead of in a form?
- How is an AI assistant placed beside content without taking over the screen?
- What do good empty states and template pickers look like?
- How do products show credit balances and what an action will cost?
- How do domain-connection screens explain DNS to non-technical people?

Look at website builders, document editors, CMS products and AI writing tools. Start with flow libraries.

### 2. Landing page: projectdelta.com

Sections: hero, how it works, who it is for, example portfolios, pricing by credits, footer.

Research questions:

- How do product sites show the product working in the hero, not a static picture?
- How do credit-based products explain pricing simply?
- How are example galleries of customer work presented?

Start with whole-site galleries, then section galleries for each part.

### 3. Public portfolio: handle.projectdelta.com and custom domains

Screens: home, work index, case study, writing, about, contact. These are themes, so research several distinct looks, not one.

Research questions:

- How do strong case studies give a one-minute summary before the detail?
- What reading aids help on long pages: progress, section index, reading time?
- How do portfolios for different professions differ (product, UX, visual design, analysis, engineering)?
- Which decorators and motion give life without hurting reading?

Start with real designers' and product people's portfolios.

### 4. Mobile app (iOS and Android)

Screens: home, capture (voice, photo, file), draft review, stats, interview practice.

Research questions:

- How do capture-first apps make adding material one tap?
- How is long AI-written text reviewed and approved on a phone?

Start with iOS screen and flow libraries.

## Where the screens are

The current screens and the first drafts of new ones are on the user's design canvas, grouped by these same four surfaces. Put reference findings on that canvas, next to the screens they inform, when the canvas is available.
