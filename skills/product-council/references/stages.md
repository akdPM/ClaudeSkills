# Stages: specialists and what the verdict must cover

Seat the five thinkers plus the specialists for the stage. Each stage also has a working skill that produces the thing being reviewed; the council reviews, it does not write.

| Stage | Specialists to add | Verdict must answer | Working skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem and product sense | Target user; market analyst | Is this worth solving, for whom, and how would we know? | `product-sense-check` |
| MVP scope | Engineer; target user | What is in, what is cut, and which assumption does version one test? | `mvp-scoping` |
| Wireframes and design | UX critic; accessibility reviewer | Which option, and what must change before it is built? | `wireframe-ideation`, `design-reference-research` |
| Technical feasibility | Architect; security; cost and operations | Can it be built, how, with what risks and rough cost? | `technical-feasibility` |
| PRD | Engineer who builds it; QA on acceptance criteria; sceptical executive | What is unclear, missing or untestable before hand-over? | `prd-writer` |
| Evals for AI features | Eval designer; red-teamer; domain expert | Do these evals catch the failures that matter, and is the bar right? | `eval-designer` |
| Pricing and credits | Finance; customer | Is the price right, is the margin safe, and what breaks with heavy users? | `pricing-and-credits` |
| Go-to-market | Marketer; community or sales lead; competitor | Who first, with what message, through which channel? | `go-to-market` |
| Product analytics | Data analyst; growth lead | Do these few events and funnels prove the product works? | `product-analytics` |

## Specialist briefs

Give each specialist one line of role and one question:

- **Target user:** speaks as the person the product is for. "Would I use this, and what would stop me?"
- **Market analyst:** "Who else does this, and why would someone switch?"
- **Engineer:** "What is hard, slow or fragile to build here?"
- **Architect:** "What is the simplest structure that will not need rebuilding in six months?"
- **Security:** "How is this abused, and what data is exposed?"
- **Cost and operations:** "What does one use cost, and what does it cost at a thousand times the volume?"
- **UX critic:** "Where does the person hesitate, and what is the page asking them to work out?"
- **Accessibility reviewer:** "Who cannot use this as designed?"
- **QA:** "Which requirement can I not write a test for?"
- **Sceptical executive:** "Why this, why now, and what are we not doing instead?"
- **Eval designer:** "Which real failure does each test catch?"
- **Red-teamer:** "How do I make the model produce something harmful, wrong or embarrassing?"
- **Domain expert:** "Is the output actually correct and useful to a practitioner?"
- **Finance:** "What is the margin on a typical user and on the heaviest user?"
- **Customer:** "Is this worth the price to me, and do I understand what I am paying for?"
- **Marketer:** "What is the one sentence, and where do these people already spend time?"
- **Community or sales lead:** "How do I reach the first hundred by hand?"
- **Competitor:** "How would I respond to this launch?"
- **Data analyst:** "Can this question be answered from the events proposed?"
- **Growth lead:** "Which number, if it moved, would prove this is working?"

To add a stage, add a row and its specialists here.
